# Third-party resources

The bundled atlas and surface files are public reference assets, not participant
images. Their upstream terms and citations remain applicable.

- **Schaefer 2018 atlas:** [CBIG repository](https://github.com/ThomasYeoLab/CBIG/tree/master/stable_projects/brain_parcellation/Schaefer2018_LocalGlobal). Copyright notice and permission are retained in [CBIG_LICENSE.md](assets/licenses/CBIG_LICENSE.md). Citation: Schaefer et al., Cerebral Cortex (2018), [doi:10.1093/cercor/bhx179](https://doi.org/10.1093/cercor/bhx179).
- **Tian S2 subcortical atlas and combined Schaefer–Tian labels:** [Melbourne Subcortex Atlas](https://github.com/yetianmed/subcortex). License retained in [Tian_LICENSE.txt](assets/licenses/Tian_LICENSE.txt). Citation: Tian et al., Nature Neuroscience (2020), [doi:10.1038/s41593-020-00711-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-020-00711-6). File sources and checksums are in [atlas provenance](assets/atlas/provenance.json).
- **fsaverage 10k surfaces and MNI152 registration-fusion coordinates:** distributed through [neuromaps](https://github.com/netneurolab/neuromaps). The upstream [license](assets/licenses/neuromaps_LICENSE) and [asset provenance](assets/surface/provenance.json) accompany these resources. Citation: Markello et al., Nature Methods (2022), [doi:10.1038/s41592-022-01625-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-022-01625-w).
- **fMRIPrep:** executed using the `nipreps/fmriprep:25.2.7` container; see [fMRIPrep documentation](https://fmriprep.org/). The container and FreeSurfer license are obtained separately.
- **Gemma 3 1B:** [model repository](https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-3-1b-pt). Weights, tokenizer files and access credentials are obtained separately under the model's terms.

The paper bibliography is provided in [references.bib](docs/references.bib).
